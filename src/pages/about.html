Who we are

Shubhkant Foundation is a Section 8 non-profit organisation committed to social development, youth empowerment, education, leadership development and community welfare.

Established with the vision of creating a more inclusive, aware and empowered society, the Foundation seeks to bridge the gap between opportunities and the communities that need them.

The Foundation believes that meaningful social transformation begins when individuals are equipped with knowledge, practical skills, awareness and the confidence to contribute positively to society. Its approach focuses on creating opportunities for learning, encouraging responsible leadership, strengthening community participation and supporting initiatives that address relevant social needs.

Who we bring together

  • Young people
  • Students
  • Professionals
  • Volunteers
  • Institutions
  • Community stakeholders

How we do it

  • Awareness programmes
  • Educational initiatives
  • Workshops
  • Outreach activities
  • Collaborative projects

The aim is to create platforms where people can learn, participate, lead and contribute to meaningful change.

As a Section 8 organisation, Shubhkant Foundation operates with a social purpose, focusing on community-oriented initiatives and the broader objective of public welfare. Its work is guided by the belief that development should extend beyond economic progress to include education, equality, responsible citizenship, personal growth and access to opportunities.

Our vision

To build an empowered, inclusive and socially responsible society where individuals have the knowledge, opportunities and confidence to realise their potential and contribute to collective progress.

Shubhkant Foundation envisions a society in which:

  • Young people are encouraged to become responsible leaders.

  • Women have greater opportunities for participation and empowerment.

  • Children receive support for holistic development.

  • Communities have access to awareness, education and meaningful engagement.

Our mission

To promote social development through education, awareness, leadership, community participation and collaborative initiatives.

The organisation seeks to:

  • Encourage youth participation, leadership and civic responsibility.
  • Promote education, practical learning and skill development.
  • Support women empowerment, equality and awareness of rights.
  • Contribute to child welfare and holistic development.
  • Facilitate legal awareness and understanding of human rights.
  • Encourage environmental responsibility and sustainable practices.
  • Build partnerships with institutions, professionals, community groups and like-minded organisations.
  • Create opportunities for volunteers and young leaders to participate in initiatives that address social needs.

Core areas of work

{{slot: work|arch|4/5|Our work in the field}}

  • Youth empowerment and leadership development

    The Foundation recognises young people as important contributors to social progress. It aims to provide opportunities for leadership, teamwork, communication, civic engagement and practical learning through initiatives that encourage young individuals to become active and responsible members of society.

  • Education and skill development

    Education is viewed as a foundation for personal growth and social transformation. Through learning initiatives, workshops and awareness programmes, the Foundation seeks to encourage knowledge beyond conventional academic settings, including life skills, communication, confidence, critical thinking and responsible decision-making.

  • Women empowerment and development

    The Foundation aims to promote awareness, equality, participation and opportunities for women through initiatives centred on empowerment, education, leadership and community engagement. Its objective is to encourage a society in which women can participate confidently and contribute to social, educational and professional development.

  • Child welfare and development

    The Foundation recognises the importance of children's well-being, learning, safety and holistic development. Initiatives in this area may focus on educational awareness, life skills, positive values, personal development and community support.

  • Social welfare and community development

    Community development is an essential part of the Foundation's approach. Through outreach activities, awareness drives, volunteer engagement and collaborative initiatives, it seeks to encourage compassion, civic responsibility and collective participation in addressing community needs.

  • Legal awareness and human rights

    Awareness of rights, responsibilities and legal processes is important for informed citizenship. The Foundation aims to support accessible legal awareness and human rights education through suitable educational initiatives, discussions and collaborations with qualified professionals.

  • Environment and sustainability

    The Foundation also recognises the importance of environmental awareness and responsible community practices. Relevant initiatives may encourage cleanliness, environmental responsibility, sustainable habits and greater public participation in protecting the environment.

Approach and working philosophy

Shubhkant Foundation believes that sustainable social impact requires more than isolated activities. It requires consistent engagement, responsible leadership, collaboration and an understanding of the communities being served.

Its approach is based on four broad principles.

  1. I

    Education

    Promoting knowledge, awareness and practical learning as tools for empowerment.

  2. II

    Participation

    Encouraging students, volunteers, professionals and community members to contribute actively to social initiatives.

  3. III

    Collaboration

    Building meaningful relationships with institutions, organisations, experts and other stakeholders to strengthen the reach and effectiveness of initiatives.

  4. IV

    Responsibility

    Emphasising ethical conduct, accountability, respect, inclusivity and responsible execution of programmes.

The Foundation seeks to encourage individuals not merely to participate in initiatives but also to develop the capacity to identify challenges, work collaboratively and contribute to practical solutions.
