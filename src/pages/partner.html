Working together

Shubhkant Foundation values collaboration as an important means of expanding the reach and effectiveness of its work.

Engagement with educational institutions, non-profit organisations, businesses, professionals, community groups and other stakeholders can create opportunities for knowledge-sharing, resource mobilisation and coordinated action.

The Foundation seeks to develop partnerships that align with its social objectives and promote mutually meaningful outcomes. It aims to approach such collaborations with clarity of purpose, professional communication and a shared commitment to the objectives of the relevant initiative.
