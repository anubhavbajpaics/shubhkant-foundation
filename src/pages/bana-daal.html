BANA DAAL

Soch. Bana. Badha.

BANA DAAL is a flagship event organised under the auspices of Shubhkant Foundation. Conceived as a platform for innovation, learning and leadership, it brings together aspiring entrepreneurs, young minds and visionary thinkers to turn potential into progress.

Youth, innovation and entrepreneurship

Shubhkant Foundation recognises that youth development extends beyond education and social awareness. It also includes entrepreneurial thinking, innovation, professional exposure and the ability to transform ideas into meaningful opportunities.

In an evolving economy, young people need platforms that encourage creativity, problem-solving, collaboration and practical understanding of how ideas can develop into viable initiatives. Entrepreneurship can contribute to employment generation, innovation, economic participation and community development.

With this perspective, the Foundation's broader vision includes encouraging platforms that bring together aspiring entrepreneurs, students, professionals, innovators and business leaders. Such platforms can support knowledge-sharing, mentorship, networking and exposure to real-world experiences.

About BANA DAAL

In a rapidly evolving global economy, young innovators often possess transformative ideas but face barriers related to business development, mentorship, scaling and networking. BANA DAAL seeks to address these challenges by creating an inclusive platform designed to inspire, educate and connect the next generation of business leaders.

The initiative is conceived to encourage young minds to explore entrepreneurial possibilities, exchange ideas, learn from experienced professionals and better understand the journey from an initial idea to practical implementation. Through discussions, networking, potential mentorship opportunities and entrepreneurial engagement, BANA DAAL aims to encourage a culture of innovation, initiative and responsible enterprise.

It complements the Foundation's commitment to youth development by connecting personal growth and leadership with entrepreneurship, professional learning and opportunity creation.

Core objectives

  • Connecting ecosystems

    Linking aspiring entrepreneurs, student innovators and startup founders with industry professionals, experienced mentors and potential investors.

  • Knowledge exchange

    Facilitating panel discussions and interactive sessions on business strategies, sustainable growth, funding awareness and technological innovation.

  • Skill and venture development

    Encouraging participants to develop business ideas through pitching opportunities, practical learning and expert feedback.

  • Fostering collaboration

    Creating opportunities for networking, interdisciplinary partnerships and professional relationships.
